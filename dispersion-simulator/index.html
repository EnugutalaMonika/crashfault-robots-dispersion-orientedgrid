Optimal Fault-Tolerant Dispersion on Oriented Grids

Round-by-round simulators for the ICDCN 2025 paper by Banerjee, Kumar and Molla. Choose one fault model. The two are never mixed.

1 · Crash-fault robots

A robot may stop at any round and disappear. Algorithm 1, Theorem 4.1: O(√n) rounds, O(log n) bits.

2 · Weak Byzantine robots

A robot may behave arbitrarily but cannot fake its ID. Theorem 4.2: O(√n) rounds, O(n log n) bits.
