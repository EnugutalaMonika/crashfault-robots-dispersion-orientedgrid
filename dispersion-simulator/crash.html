Optimal Fault-Tolerant Dispersion on Oriented Grids — Crash-Fault Algorithm Simulator

Faithful, round-by-round simulation of Algorithm 1: Oriented Grid Dispersion with Faulty Robots (Banerjee, Kumar, Molla — ICDCN 2025). Every animation step is one step of the algorithm.

1 · Grid & robots

IDs are 1…k. With k = n you get the paper's main setting; Remark 1 of the paper says any k ≤ n also works.

2 · Crash a robot

A crash happens at the start of the chosen round (before Communicate). The robot vanishes, its memory is lost, and no other robot can see it. Tip: click any robot on the grid, then press “Crash it next round”.
